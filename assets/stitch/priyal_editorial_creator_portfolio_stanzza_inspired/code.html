[ 01 / BIOGRAPHY ]

Capturing the quiet poetry of destinations and human connections.

Rooted in Kerala's lush coastlines and misty high ranges, my storytelling explores the intersection of heritage architecture, slow culinary traditions, and intentional travel.

Over the past five years, my camera has been an observational companion. Rather than breathless itineraries, I document the scent of morning cardamoms, ancestral cooking over woodfires, and dialogues with artisans who preserve ancient Indian crafts.
